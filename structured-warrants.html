Why Structured Warrants

Leverage the Market — With Managed Risk

Common Mistakes

Why 90% of Warrant Traders Lose Money

Most retail traders approach structured warrants like regular stocks — and that's where they go wrong. Here are the critical mistakes I help my students avoid:

  • 1
    Ignoring Time Decay (Theta) Warrants lose value every day. If you don't understand theta, you're fighting an invisible enemy.
  • 2
    Choosing Cheap Warrants Low price doesn't mean good value. Learn to assess warrants by delta, premium, and effective gearing — not just price.
  • 3
    Holding Too Long Warrants are short-term instruments. Holding like stocks destroys value through time decay.
  • 4
    Not Understanding the Underlying You must know the stock before trading its warrant. Technical analysis on the underlying is essential.
  • 5
    Over-Leveraging Warrants amplify both gains and losses. Without proper position sizing, one bad trade can wipe out your capital.
