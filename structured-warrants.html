Why Structured Warrants为什么学习结构性凭单

Leverage the Market — With Managed Risk杠杆撬动市场 — 风险可控

Structured warrants allow you to participate in market movements with a fraction of the capital needed for direct stock investment. But without proper knowledge, they can be costly mistakes.结构性凭单让你以直接股票投资所需资金的一小部分参与市场波动。但如果没有适当的知识，它们可能会成为代价高昂的错误。

That's why learning from someone who has traded structured products professionally at a bank and developed derivative products at Bursa Malaysia makes all the difference.这就是为什么向一位曾在银行专业交易结构性产品并在 Bursa Malaysia 开发衍生品产品的人学习会带来巨大的不同。

I don't just teach you what warrants are — I teach you how they actually work from the inside, how issuers think, and how to select the right warrant for the right trade.我不仅仅教你凭单是什么 — 我教你它们从内部如何运作、发行商如何思考，以及如何为正确的交易选择正确的凭单。

Common Mistakes残酷的事实

Why 90% of Warrant Traders Lose Money为什么90%的凭单交易者亏钱

Most retail traders approach structured warrants like regular stocks — and that's where they go wrong. Here are the critical mistakes I help my students avoid:大多数散户交易者像交易普通股票一样交易结构性凭单 — 这就是他们犯错的地方。以下是我帮助学员避免的关键错误：

  • 1
    Ignoring Time Decay (Theta)忽视时间衰减 (Theta) Warrants lose value every day. If you don't understand theta, you're fighting an invisible enemy.凭单每天都在贬值。如果你不了解 theta，你就在与一个看不见的敌人作战。
  • 2
    Choosing Cheap Warrants选择便宜的凭单 Low price doesn't mean good value. Learn to assess warrants by delta, premium, and effective gearing — not just price.低价不等于好价值。学会通过 delta、溢价和有效杠杆来评估凭单 — 而不仅仅是价格。
  • 3
    Holding Too Long持有太久 Warrants are short-term instruments. Holding like stocks destroys value through time decay.凭单是短期工具。像股票一样持有会因时间衰减而损失价值。
  • 4
    Not Understanding the Underlying不了解标的资产 You must know the stock before trading its warrant. Technical analysis on the underlying is essential.在交易凭单之前，你必须了解标的股票。对标的资产的技术分析至关重要。
  • 5
    Over-Leveraging过度杠杆 Warrants amplify both gains and losses. Without proper position sizing, one bad trade can wipe out your capital.凭单会放大收益和亏损。没有适当的仓位管理，一笔糟糕的交易就可能耗尽你的资金。
