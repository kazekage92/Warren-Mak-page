My Story我的故事

With over 30 years of experience in Malaysia's securities and derivatives markets, I've walked the journey from bank trading floors to the regulatory side at Bursa Malaysia, and now to empowering everyday Malaysians to trade smarter.

I believe that professional-grade trading knowledge shouldn't be locked behind institutional walls. That's why I founded TED Optimus — to bring the same strategies, tools, and real-market thinking I used as a professional trader to anyone willing to learn.

What makes my approach different? I teach live. Not from textbooks or slides — but from live market charts, with real trades, real mistakes, and real thinking processes. Because the market doesn't wait for you to finish a chapter.

凭借在马来西亚证券与衍生品市场超过30年的经验，我从银行交易厅走到 Bursa Malaysia 的监管层，如今致力于帮助普通马来西亚人更聪明地交易。

我相信专业级的交易知识不应被锁在机构的高墙之后。这就是我创办 TED Optimus 的原因——将我作为专业交易员所使用的策略、工具和实战思维带给每一位愿意学习的人。

我的方法有何不同？我进行实盘教学。不是从教科书或幻灯片——而是通过实时市场图表，展示真实交易、真实错误和真实思考过程。因为市场不会等你读完一章书。
