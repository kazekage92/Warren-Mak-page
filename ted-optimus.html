About Us

Building the Future of Investing for Everyday Malaysians

TED Optimus Sdn Bhd is Malaysia's first Bursa-licensed information vendor equipped with AI-powered stock analysis. Our flagship platform, Trade Wizard, helps retail investors make smarter trading decisions through simplified analytics, actionable insights, and structured education.

With a focus on accessibility and clarity, we serve a growing base of users by making professional-grade tools easy to use and understand.

Our purpose: Empower 1,000 families to achieve RM1 million in equity value by 2035. We believe every household deserves access to intelligent investment tools that were once reserved for professionals.

Through AI-powered reports, theme-based stock insights, and gamified learning, Trade Wizard shortens the learning curve and accelerates financial confidence.
