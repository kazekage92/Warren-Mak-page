## Key Quotes & Insights (Usable in Content)

### On Structured Warrants
> "Structured warrants are one of the best trading instruments on Bursa Malaysia." (结构性凭单是大马交易所中最好的交易工具之一)

> "Structured warrants are issued by third parties — qualified stockbrokers, investment banks, or financial institutions. They are cash-settled at expiry, not converted to shares."

> "Trading structured warrants is essentially trading the expected short-term price movement of the underlying asset. Buyers aim to profit from the price spread, not hold to expiry."

### On Warrant Selection
> "Avoid thinly-traded structured warrants. They trade rarely for a reason — they are not good choices among all warrants. When comparing warrant activity, compare by trading value (volume × average price)."

### On Hedging
> "The best hedging tool for HSI call warrants is HSI put warrants."

### On Day Trading
> "The most difficult yet easiest trading method is day trading — easy because you only worry about same-day events, hard because stock selection requires sniper-like precision."

### On Market Makers
> "Structured warrant and ETF market makers buy and sell stocks based on their market-making requirements from trading volumes, not based on their views of the underlying stock."
